# Baseline travel essentials

A backbone for almost any trip, in the order lists should be presented. Adjust
quantities, cut what doesn't apply, and add trip-specific items on top.

Quantities are deliberately absent here — they come from the trip.

## Documents & money
- [ ] Passport or photo ID ⚠️
- [ ] Visa / entry authorisation, if one is needed — verify with an official source ⚠️
- [ ] Boarding passes, tickets, booking confirmations
- [ ] Payment cards + a little local cash
- [ ] Travel insurance details + emergency assistance number
- [ ] Offline copies of key documents, stored separately from the originals
- [ ] House keys ⚠️

## Clothing
- [ ] Tops
- [ ] Bottoms
- [ ] Underwear
- [ ] Socks
- [ ] Sleepwear
- [ ] A warm mid layer
- [ ] Outer layer suited to the season
- [ ] Walking shoes
- [ ] Second pair of shoes

## Toiletries & medication
- [ ] Toothbrush + toothpaste
- [ ] Deodorant
- [ ] Shampoo / soap
- [ ] Razor, and any grooming kit
- [ ] Skincare + sunscreen
- [ ] Prescription medication — original labelled packaging, trip length plus a few days ⚠️
- [ ] Small first-aid kit
- [ ] Glasses / contact lenses + spares ⚠️

## Electronics
- [ ] Phone + charger ⚠️
- [ ] Power adapter for the destination
- [ ] Battery pack
- [ ] Headphones
- [ ] Any camera or laptop, with its own charger

## Activity gear
- [ ] Day bag
- [ ] Reusable water bottle
- [ ] Anything the specific activities demand — swimwear, boots, a formal outfit

## What to wear on the day
- [ ] Travel outfit — comfortable, layered
- [ ] The bulkiest coat and shoes, worn rather than packed
